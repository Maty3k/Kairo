## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Implementation plan (`docs/PLAN.md`) and the research behind it (`docs/research/`).
- `.gitignore` for IDE settings, local dogfood data and private notes.
- Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later (`LICENSE`).
- README, house rules for the coding agent (`CLAUDE.md`), security policy (`SECURITY.md`), this changelog and `.editorconfig`.
- ADR-0001: architecture and stack (`docs/adr/`).
- Milestone M0, setup and bridge: goal, gate, test card and progress (`docs/milestones/M0.md`).
- M0 development tooling: the Windows PC bootstrap script and `SETUP.md`; `kairo-devrun` (job runner and watch agent); the `kairo-hello` test window; `kairo-cli` (`ping`, `keys`); the Mac-side `tools/mac/pc` tool; CI.
- A CI job that runs the bootstrap end to end on a fresh Windows runner, three times (#2).
- The bootstrap switches off Visual Studio's optional usage data before installing the Build Tools, and its DONE screen repeats the yellow warnings (#4).
- Milestone M0 closed on 2026-10-23: all six gates passed (gate report in `docs/milestones/M0.md`).
- Milestone M1, test instruments: goal, gate and progress (`docs/milestones/M1.md`).
- `kairo-core` media maths: the clock that ties output ticks and audio samples to QPC time, the frame pacer, the `wgc-probe` trace format with its offline replay, and cadence statistics (#10).
- `kairo-core` replay ring and clip cut: keyframe-indexed packets evicted a whole GOP at a time, gapless audio tracks, pins for saves in flight, and cutting a clip that starts on a keyframe, never spans two encoder epochs and starts its audio one AAC packet early (#11).
- `kairo-core` audio aligner: places each capture source on the 48 kHz timeline, follows its clock drift with a PI loop steering the rubato 5.0.1 resampler, and splices over gaps, overlaps, pauses and reported losses with 2 ms fades, never behind the mixer (#12).
- `kairo-core` mixer: stem tracks clipped for their encoders; the mix track as the sum of the stems in the mix times their gains (ramped across a block), through a look-ahead limiter that holds it under −1 dBFS whatever the input; peak and RMS meters; and a mixer catch-up capped at one second (#13).
- `kairo-mock-engine`, the Mac's stand-in for the engine: `kairo-engine.sock` in the per-user temp folder with the engine's framing, the `engine.hello` handshake first (refusing another major version) and `engine.shutdown` (#15).
- The design of `kairo-verify`, the testbed and the voice-tone process, with its research notes (`docs/research/16-verify-testbed/`) (#17).
- `kairo-core` pattern v1, the test signal the testbed draws and `kairo-verify` reads back: a block barcode with a CRC-16, whitened so no cell holds a level for long, moving bars, a grey ramp, a flash, drifting patches, a load band, a motion variant without barcodes or flash, −30 dBFS tone bursts, BT.709 and 4:2:0 maths, and the PQ and BT.2390 curves for HDR patches (#18).
- `kairo-core` testbed and tone logs (ground truth written by `testbed.exe` and `kairo-tone.exe` in QPC time) and per-step cadence in any content unit at any output rate, with the judder-step rule (#19).
- `kairo-verify`, the clip checker, with its own MP4, H.264 and AAC header parser and the structure checks: moov first, no edit list, `colr`, the parameter sets, CFR, start alignment, no reordering, IDR at 0, the closed 1 s GOP, the track layout, AAC LC at 48 kHz, durations, one encoder session, bitrate, and the export limits that need no decoding; profiles, a versioned JSON report naming the file only, exit codes, and `boxes` (#20).
- `kairo-display`, the monitor by its device path and the adapter by its LUID for M1's Windows programs (display configuration API and DXGI: GDI name, rectangle, refresh rate, HDR state, mirroring, adapter index and PCI ids), and `kairo-harness displays` with `pc harness displays`, which keeps what it finds and the monitor aliases in `.dogfood/pc/` and refuses an alias whose GDI number moved or whose display is mirrored (#21).
- `kairo-verify mutate`, which makes a bad clip from a good one with one box-level change: moov after the media, an identity edit list, a wrong or missing `colr`, a second default track, or one uneven frame. It rebuilds `moov` with every size recomputed and moves every chunk offset with its media, refuses clips with other file offsets, and never replaces a file; its tests prove each output equals the same defect built from scratch on eleven file layouts and fails only the checks that defect names (#22).
- `kairo-verify` reads pattern v1 in decoded pictures: a decoder interface with a synthetic backend and a CLI backend that spawns a given FFmpeg (`--ffmpeg`, development only), and the checks `decode.clean` (video), `content.barcode`, `cadence.drops_dups`, `cadence.judder` (against an ideal every-vblank delivery replayed through `kairo-core`'s pacer) and `content.flash`. A clip whose testbed frame never advances fails, a check that can't be computed is an error rather than a pass, and the CLI backend counts FFmpeg's warnings, since a concealed frame is only a warning. A VideoToolbox-encoded clip passes them on the Mac (#24).
- `kairo-verify` judges the bars, the ramp and the grey patches: `content.bars` (every frame's worst plane against the analytic reference, unreadable frames scored against their likely frame numbers), `content.colour_matrix` (BT.601, full range and swapped chroma), `content.bars_lines`, `content.levels` and `hdr.curve_levels` (PQ with BT.2390, or clipped). The line rule leaves the 16 px at each side, where scrolling brings new content in, out of its columns, and the ramp's chroma only warns in exports. Measured on the Mac: an untagged raw input makes FFmpeg convert BT.601 to BT.709 before encoding, which these checks catch, and a VideoToolbox 1080p clip at 25/40 Mbps passes the `test` profile's 4-code line rule (#25).
- `kairo-verify synth`, the test pattern at a clip's size and rate for an encoder: raw NV12, or a lossless I_PCM H.264 stream (High profile, CAVLC, every macroblock's samples raw, an IDR every `--gop` frames) for the CI corpus; the defects drop, dup, judder, corrupt-bars, bars-sparse and unreadable; HDR patches; and the clip's `expect.json`, whose `cabac: false` only the `test` profile accepts. Measured on the Mac: FFmpeg's stream copy of an I_PCM clip decodes to `synth`'s exact frames, each defect fails only its own checks, and the raw H.264 input needs `-r 60`, since `-framerate 60` alone leaves uneven frame durations (#26).
- `kairo-verify` judges the audio: each track's (L+R)/2 decoded at its own channel count (the CLI decoder too), the beeps found by a demodulated 1 ms envelope, each judged on its own steady part so a louder sound nearby can't move it, and refined by the carrier's phase on a cycle chosen from both ends of the beep, which AAC smears less than its start, and `av.offset` (every beep within 40 ms of its picture, up to half a second off, and no flash without its beep on a track that carries the game; voice-only and mic-only tracks beep by beep through the Mix), `av.offset_tight` (M3 task 9, `--av-tight` with `engine` only), `av.inter_track` (±0.5 ms, however far apart), `audio.tone_snr` (≥35 dB and ±1 Hz, overlapping tones fitted jointly), `audio.routing` (each tone's narrowband level against what the track should carry, a track that stops early heard as silence) and `audio.cue` (an FFT matched filter, `--cue FILE.wav`), plus `decode.clean`'s rule of 1,024 samples per AAC packet. Audio a decoder can't give back is an error, never silence. A synthetic clip now passes the `test` profile, each audio defect of research 16 §7.3 fails only its own check, and an AAC-muxed clip reads within a millisecond of its pictures on the Mac, where a track one AAC packet late fails (#27).
- `kairo-verify synth --wav-dir DIR` writes the clip's audio as research 16 §7.1 lays it out: `mix.wav`, `game.wav`, `mic.wav` (mono) and `voice.wav` at 48 kHz, the game beep on each second with its flash, the mic marker 250 ms after it and the voice beep 500 ms after it, all at −30 dBFS, and the expect file then lists the four tracks. Five audio defects change only the WAVs: `av50` (2,400 samples of silence first), `swap-game-voice`, `cue`, `leak` (the voice beep at −40 dBFS in Game) and `noisy` (−55 dBFS RMS in Game). Measured on the Mac with FFmpeg's AAC: the good clip reads −0.02 ms with a 42.1 dB worst tone SNR, `av50` reads +49.98 ms, and each defect fails only its own check (#28).

### Changed

- The plan pins Tauri 2.12.5 with `tauri-build` 2.7.4 and drops the updater and process plugins (#3).
- The PC's FFmpeg pin moves to BtbN's 2026-09-30 month-end build of 9.0.2 (`n9.0.2-17-g2a571b6068`), which BtbN keeps for two years, and the bootstrap's CI job fails if the pinned build lacks an encoder or filter (#14).
- The plan records decision 37: the owner accepts M1's exit-rule counting (#16).
- `pc build` runs cargo as the owner's account instead of in the elevated SSH session: a hidden `\Kairo\Run` job at below-normal priority (job files gain `priority`) that builds into `C:\kairo\build`. The elevated side no longer writes, deletes or blindly reads anything in the folders the owner's account can write (kairo-devrun prunes old job logs instead), `pc deploy` copies only Kairo's programs that the owner's account made, `pc cli` runs `kairo-cli` as a job, and `pc doctor` and the bootstrap read tool versions from files instead of running programs from the owner's profile with administrator rights; the bootstrap's repair no longer walks the owner-writable folders. The elevated scripts now fix PowerShell's module path before their first command (CI checks it), since PowerShell 7 would otherwise load a module from the owner's Documents first; Git never comes from the PATH and reads no per-user files; `pc sync` logs into the admin-only `jobs\`; and `pc ssh`, an interactive elevated shell, is gone (#23).

### Fixed

- The bootstrap makes Administrators the owner of `C:\kairo` and everything in it except `logs` and `state` (#2).
- `SETUP.md` now matches what the bootstrap and the undo script do (#3).
